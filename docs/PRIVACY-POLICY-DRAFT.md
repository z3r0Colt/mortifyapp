# Mortify privacy policy

The policy now lives as a public page: `public/privacy-policy/index.html`, served at https://mortify.gentleking.org/privacy-policy/. The outside-app deletion page is `public/delete-account/index.html`, at https://mortify.gentleking.org/delete-account/. Edit those files, not this one.

Before linking either page from a store listing, replace the remaining bracketed fields in the policy: effective date, legal operator name, provider backup and log retention, and the legal basis, international transfer and user-rights wording your jurisdiction requires.
