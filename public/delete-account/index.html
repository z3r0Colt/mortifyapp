Delete your Mortify account

In the app

Open Mortify while connected to the internet. Go to Settings, then Account, and choose Delete my account. Your account is removed at once.

Without the app

Send an email to mortify@gentleking.org from the email address you sign in with, asking us to delete your account. If you write from another address, we will ask you to confirm that the account is yours before we delete it.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes everything in it: your email address, preferences, reading place and Flee records, your encrypted journal, confessions and reflections, your profile, brethren links, messages, shared events, notification addresses and settings. It cannot be undone.

A brother or sister may still remember a message you sent. Copies in our providers' backups may last for a short time afterward, as the privacy policy explains.
